How it works

A simple workflow from listing to distribution

1

Share the opportunity brief

Send the role, programme, or opportunity details with the source link, deadline, who it is for, and how people should apply.

2

APPLY! packages it for clarity

We reshape the information into a cleaner format so the audience can quickly understand the offer, the fit, and the next steps.

3

Distribute through the right surfaces

WhatsApp remains the main delivery layer. The website supports trust and public visibility, and LinkedIn can support broader public proof.

What to send

The cleaner the brief, the stronger the post

To move quickly, the team should have the core facts up front. That helps reduce back-and-forth and makes it easier to produce a useful, trustworthy post.

Recommended information

  • Official source link
  • Opportunity title and organisation name
  • Deadline or application window
  • Who it is for
  • Application steps or destination
  • Important documents or requirements

Best-use cases

  • You want the opportunity explained more clearly for applicants.
  • You need a youth-facing audience in Ghana.
  • You want stronger trust signals than a poster alone can provide.
  • You want distribution that feels more useful than generic reposting.

Why this matters

APPLY! exists because many strong opportunities lose impact when the information is unclear, late, or hard to trust. Better packaging helps better applicants act.
