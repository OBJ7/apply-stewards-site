# APPLY! Stewards Product Strategy

## Core position

APPLY! Stewards is a WhatsApp-first opportunity distribution and trust brand for students and young graduates in Ghana.

The website is not the primary product. Its job is to:

- build trust
- convert partners
- provide a clean public archive of selected opportunities
- send people back into the WhatsApp channel for regular updates

## Problem

High-value opportunities are often shared too late, too vaguely, or through low-trust formats. Many students and graduates see an opening but still lack the clarity to act quickly:

- Who is this for?
- Is this credible?
- What do I need?
- Where do I apply?

## User groups

### Primary community users

- SHS leavers
- university students
- NSS personnel
- recent graduates

### Primary business users

- recruiters
- employers
- scholarship and fellowship teams
- training providers
- programme managers
- institutions seeking youth reach in Ghana

## Channel roles

### WhatsApp

- Main distribution channel
- Fastest way to reach the community
- Best place for urgency, reminders, and momentum

### Website

- Trust layer
- Partnership conversion layer
- Informational layer
- Lightweight opportunity hub

### LinkedIn

- Public credibility layer
- Partner acquisition layer
- Proof of activity and outcomes

## Product principles

1. WhatsApp first, web second.
2. Clarity beats feature count.
3. Trust signals must be obvious.
4. The homepage should sell the mission, not explain every possible future tool.
5. Human review stays in the publishing loop for high-trust content.

## What the public website should do now

### Homepage

- Explain what APPLY! is
- Show proof that it is active and trusted
- Give one live featured opportunity as proof of work
- Make `Partner with us` and `Join WhatsApp` the clearest calls to action

### Opportunities page

- Hold the active opportunity experience
- Show the featured verified opportunity
- Show the separate member-advertised lane

### Partners page

- Explain who APPLY! reaches
- Explain what partners can share
- Explain how listings are packaged and distributed
- Convert enquiries

### Submit page

- Allow community and partner submissions into the member-advertised review lane

## What not to prioritise now

- a full multi-feature platform
- heavy user accounts and dashboards
- gamification systems
- a broad AI planner in the main navigation
- too many different stories on the homepage

## Near-term roadmap

### Phase 1: tighten the story

- simplify homepage messaging
- add dedicated partners page
- keep one featured opportunity on homepage
- move extra browsing behaviour to the opportunities page

### Phase 2: strengthen operations

- one structured intake record for every opportunity
- one workflow that generates WhatsApp, web, and LinkedIn outputs
- separate verified, member-advertised, and future sponsored lanes

### Phase 3: improve usefulness

- searchable public archive
- better source labels and timestamps
- application support content such as document checklists and how-to-apply guides

### Phase 4: test guidance carefully

- small pilot for 30- or 90-day action planning
- connect guidance to real opportunities already in the APPLY! system
- expand only if there is strong usage

## Success metrics

- partner enquiry volume
- partner-to-published-listing conversion
- join-channel clicks from the website
- published verified opportunities per week
- member submissions approved
- success stories and testimonial count

## Working rule

If a new idea does not make one of these better:

- trust
- partner conversion
- clarity
- distribution efficiency

it should probably wait.
