Career planner

Guidance, not just opportunities.

This is where the next layer of APPLY! Stewards will grow: helping someone move from “I need direction” to “I know what to do this week.” It stays separate from the landing page on purpose so the homepage can stay clear while this becomes a more useful working tool.

What will launch here first

The first version should stay small and practical. It does not need to be a giant app on day one to be genuinely helpful.

  • A short profile that captures where someone is now and where they want to go.
  • A 30-, 60-, or 90-day action plan tailored to their goal.
  • Simple weekly check-ins that keep them moving.
  • Opportunity recommendations pulled from the APPLY! database.
How it could work

A lightweight guidance loop for people who need structure

The goal is not to overwhelm people with “AI features.” The goal is to make steady progress easier, clearer, and more motivating.

1

Map the starting point

Users describe their background, current stage, interests, constraints, and the kind of opportunity or career direction they want to pursue.

2

Build a realistic plan

The planner turns that into a simple next-step roadmap: skills to build, documents to prepare, opportunities to watch, and actions for the next few weeks.

3

Stay consistent

Check-ins, reminders, and future streaks can help members keep going instead of falling off after one burst of motivation.

Design guardrails

Keep it helpful, not heavy

Start with one strong use case

90-day planning for students and early-career graduates is enough to create real value before adding streaks, dashboards, or deeper personalization.

Tie guidance to real opportunities

The planner becomes much stronger when it recommends actions against live APPLY! opportunities instead of giving generic advice in isolation.

Build trust with simple language

The tone should feel like a mentor helping someone move forward, not a robot flooding them with career jargon.

Keep the community close

The planner should support the WhatsApp community, not replace it. The human and community layer is part of what makes APPLY! special.
