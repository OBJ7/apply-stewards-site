What it will help with

Support for the application work people keep postponing

The Help Desk is not replacing the free WhatsApp channel. It is the next layer for people who want more structured help after they find a relevant opportunity.

Documents

CV and cover letter support

Simple reviews, stronger positioning, and clearer alignment between the applicant and the opportunity.

Planning

Application checklists

Break an opportunity into documents, deadlines, links, requirements, and next steps people can actually follow.

Follow-through

Deadline reminders

Light accountability so promising opportunities do not disappear into screenshots, chats, and forgotten tabs.

How it connects

From opportunity post to stronger submission

The long-term APPLY product is a pipeline: discover the right opportunity, understand the fit, prepare the application, and follow through before the deadline.

1
Find the opportunity

Verified posts and member-advertised listings continue through the free public surfaces.

2
Understand the fit

APPLY summaries explain audience, requirements, deadline, and official source link.

3
Prepare documents

The Help Desk pilot supports CVs, cover letters, checklists, and application readiness.

4
Track and submit

A later planning layer can help applicants keep momentum and avoid missing deadlines.
